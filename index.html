How do rewards and payouts work?

Clips earn points based on views and engagement. Once your points balance crosses the in-app payout threshold, you can redeem it for a cash payout through the app's supported payment method. Payout eligibility and rates may change — check the app for current terms.

Someone else is in my footage — is that okay to post?

Use the in-app blur tool to obscure faces and number plates before posting where possible. Recording and publishing laws vary by location, and it's your responsibility to make sure your upload complies with the law where you filmed it. See our Terms of Service below for more.

How do I report a clip or request footage be removed?

Use the "Report" option on any clip in-app, or email support@newiracraft.com with a link to the clip and the reason for your request. If you appear in someone else's footage and want it reviewed, include that in your message.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email support@newiracraft.com with the subject "Delete My Account" from your registered email address. We'll complete the deletion within 30 days.

The app isn't working — what should I do?

Try closing and reopening KarenDashcams, and make sure you're on the latest version from the Play Store. Still stuck? Email us with your device model and OS version.
